1. Acceptance

By using RewardBot or interacting with its features, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the bot. Discord’s own terms, policies, and rules also apply to your use of Discord.

2. The service

RewardBot provides tools for Discord communities to track virtual currency, configure a currency name, and submit withdrawal requests through tickets. Features may change or become unavailable. Currency is a server-specific virtual balance maintained by the bot; it is not legal tender, a bank deposit, or a guarantee of real-world value.

3. Virtual currency and balances

  • Server owners configure the currency name and the rules for earning, spending, and requesting withdrawals.
  • Balances are records within the bot and do not represent funds held by the bot operator.
  • Do not exploit bugs, manipulate balances, or attempt unauthorized balance changes.
  • Server owners should communicate their reward rules clearly and apply them consistently.
  • We cannot guarantee balances will always be available or preserved if a server stops using the bot or the service is interrupted.

4. Withdrawals and tickets

A withdrawal command may create a ticket for authorized staff to review. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval, payout, or a particular processing time.

  • Staff may approve or reject requests according to the server’s rules and available resources.
  • Staff should reject a request when it is declined.
  • Only after the real-world payout has been completed should authorized staff use “Mark Payout” or the success action.
  • When marked complete, the bot may deduct the approved amount from the member’s virtual balance.
  • Staff are responsible for verifying an external payment before marking it complete.

For an incorrect status or disputed deduction, contact the server owner or authorized staff and provide the ticket details.

5. Server owner and staff responsibilities

Server owners are responsible for currency settings, reward rules, staff permissions, payout methods, and decisions. Only trusted, authorized staff should approve, reject, or mark withdrawals complete. The bot operator does not independently verify external payments and is not the custodian of users’ funds.
