What it does

Everything important.
Nothing needlessly complicated.

A focused rewards workflow for server owners and members — with the community in control of its own rules.

Your currency, your rules

Each server can choose a currency name and configure how members use their virtual balance.

Withdrawals in a ticket

Members request a withdrawal and a ticket gives the authorized team a place to review it.

Completion that means something

Staff should mark a payout complete only after processing it. The bot can then deduct the virtual amount.

Decisions stay with staff

Server owners set the rules, manage permissions, and decide whether requests meet their requirements.

Built for communities

Virtual balances belong to a server’s reward setup, rather than being presented as real-world money held by the bot.

Clear expectations

Privacy and usage terms explain the basics for members, server owners, and staff.

The workflow

From request to resolution.

Three clear steps keep the process easy to understand.

01 / REQUEST

A member asks to withdraw

The bot creates a ticket with the requested virtual currency amount.

02 / REVIEW

Staff make a decision

Authorized staff review the ticket and approve or reject it under the server’s rules.

03 / CONFIRM

The outcome is recorded

After an actual payout, staff mark it complete. The bot may then deduct the virtual balance.
